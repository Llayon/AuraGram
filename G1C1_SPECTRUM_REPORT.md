## Decision

The winner-only graph does discard some filter-supported continuities. Retaining every eligible axis recovers a route but also increases ambiguity and joins negative-context structures. Four local observed pixels do not solve strand ownership. This experiment therefore does not justify changing Length. Any future implementation needs independent evidence of the same strand and a real free endpoint; increasing extension or relaxing floors is unsupported by these results.
