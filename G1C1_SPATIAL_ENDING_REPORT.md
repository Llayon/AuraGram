### 2026-11-18: lower bound for a complete 36 px native connection

Revisited the inconclusive union-of-half-planes bound. Distance to each finite lid segment changes by at most the displacement. A segment whose starting distance exceeds the nearest segment's by delta cannot become closest in less than delta/2 pixels. For each segment, take the maximum of that necessary displacement and the distance to its signed 1.5 px gate half-plane, then take the minimum across segments. For a frozen tip polygon, subtract the radius of a disk enclosing all its vertices. This bounds the path length from any point in the region to the existing signed gate. It gives no guarantee of reachability when the bound is at or below 36. Ambiguous normal orientation near collinearity is treated conservatively.
